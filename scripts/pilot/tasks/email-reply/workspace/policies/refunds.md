# Fernhill Home - Refunds, Replacements & Billing

Internal guide for the support team. Last updated 2026-09-01.
Quote these terms to customers. Do not offer anything that is not listed here.

## 1. Returns (change of mind)

- Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- A $5.95 return-shipping fee is deducted from the refund. The fee does not apply to damaged or defective items (see section 2).
- The refund goes to the original payment method within 5 business days after the return reaches our warehouse.

## 2. Damaged or defective items

- The customer must report the damage within 14 days of delivery and send at least one photo of the damage. We cannot approve a claim until we have a photo.
- Once the claim is approved, the customer chooses one of:
  - **Replacement**: we ship a new item within 2 business days of approval. Replacement shipping is free; the customer pays nothing for it.
  - **Refund**: the item price goes back to the original payment method within 5 business days of approval.
- The customer does not need to send the damaged item back. They can recycle or dispose of it.

## 3. Late deliveries

- Express shipping ($12.95) comes with a guaranteed delivery date. If an Express order arrives after that date, refund the Express shipping fee in full to the original payment method within 5 business days.
- Standard shipping dates are estimates only. We do not compensate for late Standard orders.

## 4. Billing errors and duplicate charges

- If an order was charged more than once, confirm the duplicate in the payments dashboard and reverse it the same day.
- Reversals take 3-5 business days to appear on the customer's card statement, depending on their bank.
- Pending authorizations are not duplicate charges. They drop off on their own within 7 days; we cannot speed this up.

## 5. Goodwill

- Store credit, discount codes and other goodwill gestures need a supervisor's approval. Do not offer them in a reply.
