## 3. Payments / checkout

We spent a while on whether to keep maintaining our own checkout form or move to
Stripe's hosted checkout. Marco's view: our form is the source of most payment
bugs and PCI questions, and hosted checkout gets us Apple Pay for free. Lena
worried about losing the branded look, but Stripe lets us set colors and the
logo, which she's fine with.

- **Decision:** we're switching to Stripe hosted checkout for v2.4. The custom
  card form gets removed.

ACTION: Marco to add rate limiting to the public booking API before the load
test on Thu Nov 12. Last month one studio's embedded widget hammered us with
retries and took down booking for everyone.

## 4. Studio pilot

Two studios (Harbor Yoga and Pulse Pilates) agreed to try the new waitlist
feature before general release. Aiko has been the main contact with both. Jonah
mentioned that support keeps getting confused tickets about the waitlist
notification emails ("am I booked or not?"), and since the pilot will make that
worse before it gets better, he's going to pull the last 30 days of
waitlist-related tickets and send Marco a summary by Monday Nov 9, so Marco can
see which email states confuse people before anyone touches the templates.
Everyone agreed the wording of those emails needs another pass at some point,
but Lena wants to see the ticket data first.

Aiko volunteered to schedule the pilot kickoff call with both studios. It will
probably land the week of Nov 16, depending on their availability.
